# Sharing chats and downloading results

> Give others access to a conversation or download a generated document.

## Share a chat or replay

Sharing a chat with an email address gives that person access to the conversation. It does not email them a transcript. A copied private chat link works only for people who already have access.

To send a replay through another app, the conversation owner can open **Share → Public access → Share now**, then copy the replay link. Anyone with that link can watch the replay, so enable public access only when you intend to make the conversation viewable that way.

## Download generated results

Ask Julius to create the document you need, then use the download reference for the generated file. Julius supports generating downloadable documents, including PDFs.

Full-chat export as PDF, DOCX or Jupyter Notebook is no longer available. A generated document is an output from your request, not a restored full-chat export or a guaranteed copy of the complete conversation.

If sharing or downloading fails, contact [team@julius.ai](mailto:team@julius.ai) with what happened and any useful link or screenshot.
