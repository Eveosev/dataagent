Spotter
The AI Analyst You Can Actually Trust
An analyst for everyone on your team, so every 
decision is backed by data
REQUEST A DEMOTRY SPOTTER NOW
Trusted by data-driven enterprises
Built for Business Users, 
Governed By Your Data Team
Meet the AI analyst your data team can govern and your business users will actually use. It works alongside your dashboards, answers across structured and unstructured data, and ships inside your product in no time.
Get the Same Answer, Every Time
Spotter turns your questions into search tokens and complex, multi-step ones into AgentQL. Then it compiles them into identical governed SQL on every run. 
Every Business User Gets An Analyst
Business users ask in plain language and get answers they can act on, without writing SQL. Spotter works right inside your Liveboards, so anyone can ask for a follow-up.
Ship Faster With Confidence
Embed Spotter in your product under your brand and scoped to each customer. Your customers get a governed AI analyst, and your product teams skip building and maintaining the DIY stack.
Sharper Insights From All of Your Data
Spotter reads your warehouse and the context around it, from Slack threads to Confluence pages, in the same answer. It learns, so answers get sharper the more your teams use it.

Gartner Critical Capabilities

From the Quick Answer 
To the Hard Why
One AI analyst on all your governed data, from the first answer to the 
deepest investigation, wherever your team works.
Translates Business Questions Into Data Analysis
Business questions no longer wait on an analyst. Ask why margin dropped in the West. Spotter runs the analysis, from quick lookups to drill-downs, forecasting, change analysis, and regression, and shows every step.
Gets Sharper With Every Conversation
Spotter keeps every correction you make in the conversation. It learns from your data models, your conversations, and the Liveboards. It shows exactly what it applied to each answer, so you can review or update it.
Governs Every Answer With The Semantic Layer
Built on ThoughtSpot's semantic layer, Snowflake semantic views, or dbt semantic models. Change a definition once, and every dashboard and answer picks it up.
Connects To Any Data, Anywhere
Live query across 30+ sources, including Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, and more. Your data stays where it is, zero copy. Spotter also pulls in context from Confluence, Slack, and other business apps.
Every Team Gets Their Own AI Analyst
Spotter Analysts turns Spotter into a specialist for each team. Pick the certified data models, tools, and instructions, and a Supply Chain Analyst answers in supply chain terms.
Work With Spotter, Anywhere You Work
Shows up in Slack, Microsoft Teams, ThoughtSpot Mobile, Liveboards, Claude, and ChatGPT like an analyst on your team. Message it, get the answer in the thread, and let it file the follow-up.
Production-Ready, Enterprise-Grade
Security at Every Layer
Row- and column-level security enforced at the query engine, SSO and SAML through your existing provider, and full audit logs on every query and action.
Compliance, Certified
SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA-ready from day one. Deploy in your region, with dedicated single-tenant and customer-managed key options.
Bring Your 
Own LLM
Connect the model you already trust and govern. Conversation monitoring shows what was asked, what was answered, and how it was produced.
Native on iOS and Android
Spotter with memory and rich data visualizations runs natively on iOS and Android. Not a cut-down view of the desktop product.
Zero Token 
Cost
Spotter is priced per seat, per customer, or per warehouse query, not per token. Your warehouse compute stays a separate bill with your provider.
Build Your Own Spotter
Make Spotter work the way your organization does. Encode your definitions, policies, and approval rules once. Everything stays inside the guardrails you set.
