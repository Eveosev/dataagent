# uscis-aao / whole

## 制度原文

U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) may adjust the status of a U nonimmigrant to that of an LPR if, among other requirements, they have been physically present in the United States for a continuous period of three years since the date of their admission as a U nonimmigrant, and they establish that their continued presence in the United States is justified on humanitarian grounds, to ensure family unity, or is otherwise in the public interest. Section 245(m) of the Act.

## 事实

The petitioner is a native and citizen of Mexico who was granted U-1 nonimmigrant status from June 2019 through June 2023. She timely filed Form I-485, Application to Adjust Status, in February 2023. The Nebraska Service Center issued a request for evidence (RFE) asking for proof of continuous physical presence, a statement of continuous physical presence, all passport pages valid during her U-1 status, and explanations for several questions on Part 8 of the I-485 (questions 16, 25–29, and 77) that she had answered “yes.” The petitioner responded with evidence of continuous physical presence, a written statement of continuous physical presence, and passport pages, but did not provide the requested explanations for the yes answers. The Service Center issued a second RFE; the petitioner responded with a document from the Justice Center Court. The Director denied the application, finding the responses did not include the required explanations for the affirmative answers and therefore did not satisfy the documentation requests. On appeal, the petitioner submitted an appeal cover letter, the missing explanations for why she answered “yes” to questions 16, 25–29, and 77 in Part 8, and two letters pertaining to her criminal records. She filed the appeal to contest the denial of her I-485 and to have the newly submitted explanations and documents considered.
