## The Deepest Journey: Exploring Earth's Interior

### 1. Essential Problem:

The question asks about the deepest point reached by humans or technology within the Earth, whether on land or under the sea. 

### 2. Relevant Information:

To address this, we need to consider different categories of exploration:

* **On Land:**
    * Deepest cave: Krubera Cave (-2,197 meters)
    * Deepest borehole: Kola Superdeep Borehole (-12,262 meters)
* **Under the Sea:**
    * Deepest manned submersible dive: Challenger Deep (-10,928 meters)
    * Deepest uncrewed submersible dive: Hadal Exploration Program (-10,994 meters)

### 3. Draft Answer:

The deepest point reached by humans on land is the bottom of the **Krubera Cave** in Georgia, at a depth of **2,197 meters** below the surface. However, the deepest point reached overall is the **Challenger Deep** in the Mariana Trench, at a depth of **10,928 meters** below sea level. This was achieved by the manned submersible **Trieste** in 1960.

While humans haven't gone deeper than Challenger Deep, uncrewed submersibles have reached even further. During the **Hadal Exploration Program**, an autonomous vehicle descended to **10,994 meters** in the Kermadec Trench.

As for drilling, the deepest borehole is the **Kola Superdeep Borehole** in Russia, reaching a depth of **12,262 meters**. However, it's important to note that this doesn't penetrate the Earth's mantle, which begins at around 30 kilometers deep.

**Additional Points:**

* The Earth's radius is approximately 6,371 kilometers.
* Exploring the Earth's interior remains a significant challenge due to extreme pressure, temperature, and the unknown.
* Technological advancements continue to push the boundaries of exploration, allowing us to probe deeper into the Earth's mysteries.
