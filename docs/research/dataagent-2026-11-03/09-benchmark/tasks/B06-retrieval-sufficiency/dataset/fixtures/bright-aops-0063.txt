##  Problem Identification:

The problem asks us to determine the ratio in which point $E$ divides side $BC$ of $\triangle ABC$. We are given that point $F$ divides side $AC$ in the ratio $1:2$ and that $G$ is the midpoint of $BF$. 

##  Reasoning and Information:

To solve this problem, we need to utilize the concept of similar triangles. Since $G$ is the midpoint of $BF$, we know that $BG = GF$. We can also see that $\triangle ABG$ and $\triangle ACG$ share an angle at $A$ and have a right angle at $B$ and $C$ respectively. Therefore, by AA Similarity, we know that $\triangle ABG \sim \triangle ACG$. 

Furthermore, since $F$ divides $AC$ in the ratio $1:2$, we know that $AF:FC = 1:2$. This implies that $AC = 3AF$. 

##  Drafting an Answer:

Using the similarity of triangles, we can write the following proportion:

$$\frac{BG}{AG} = \frac{AB}{AC}$$

Substituting $BG = GF$ and $AC = 3AF$, we get:

$$\frac{GF}{AG} = \frac{AB}{3AF}$$

Simplifying, we get:

$$\frac{1}{3} = \frac{AB}{AF}$$

Cross-multiplying, we get:

$$AF = 3AB$$

Now, consider $\triangle AEB$ and $\triangle AFC$. Both triangles share an angle at $A$ and have a right angle at $E$ and $F$ respectively. Therefore, by AA Similarity, we have:

$$\triangle AEB \sim \triangle AFC$$

Using the similarity of triangles, we can write the following proportion:

$$\frac{AE}{AF} = \frac{AB}{AC}$$

Substituting $AF = 3AB$, we get:

$$\frac{AE}{3AB} = \frac{AB}{3AF}$$

Simplifying, we get:

$$\frac{AE}{AB} = \frac{1}{3}$$

Therefore, point $E$ divides side $BC$ in the ratio $\boxed{1:3}$.
