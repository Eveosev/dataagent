## Essential Problem:

The essential problem is to determine the phase angle ϕ(z,y,x) in the annual harmonic equation for temperature and salinity, given 4D gridded data (time, depth, lat, lon) with monthly time steps.

## Step-by-Step Approach:

1. **Data Preparation:**
    - Ensure consistent units for temperature and salinity across the entire dataset.
    - Check for missing data and handle it appropriately (e.g., interpolation, removal).

2. **Calculating the Annual Mean:**
    - For each grid point (z,y,x), calculate the monthly mean values across all years. This will provide a reference point for the annual cycle.

3. **Detrending:**
    - For each grid point (z,y,x), remove the long-term trend from the data by subtracting the monthly mean values from the original data. This step helps isolate the annual variability.

4. **Fourier Transform:**
    - Apply a Fourier Transform to the detrended data for each grid point (z,y,x). This will decompose the signal into its constituent frequencies.

5. **Identifying the Annual Peak:**
    - In the power spectrum obtained from the Fourier Transform, identify the peak corresponding to the 12-month period (annual frequency). This peak represents the amplitude (A(z,y,x)) of the annual harmonic.

6. **Phase Angle Calculation:**
    - The phase angle ϕ(z,y,x) can be calculated using the following equation:
        - ϕ(z,y,x) = arctan(Im[F(z,y,x,12)] / Re[F(z,y,x,12)])
        - Where:
            - F(z,y,x,12) is the complex Fourier coefficient at the annual frequency (12 months).
            - Im[] and Re[] represent the imaginary and real parts of the complex number, respectively.

7. **Reconstructing the Annual Harmonic:**
    - Using the calculated amplitude A(z,y,x) and phase angle ϕ(z,y,x), you can reconstruct the annual harmonic for each grid point using the original equation:
        - Var(t,z,y,x) = A(z,y,x) * cos[(2πt/12 + ϕ(z,y,x)]
