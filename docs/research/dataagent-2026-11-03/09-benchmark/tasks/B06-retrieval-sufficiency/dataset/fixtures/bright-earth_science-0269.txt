2. **Clinoptilolite:** This is the most common authigenic zeolite, often found in sedimentary rocks. It is formed through the alteration of volcanic glass or the diagenetic transformation of other minerals, including biogenic opal.

4. **Bohrmann et al. (1989) hypothesis:** This hypothesis proposes that the silicate phase of some clinoptilolites originates from the diagenetic degradation of biogenic opal, specifically from radiolarians.
