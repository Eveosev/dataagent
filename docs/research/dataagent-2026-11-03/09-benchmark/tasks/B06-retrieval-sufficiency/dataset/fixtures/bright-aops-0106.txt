- $CN = \frac{1}{2} \cdot BC = 2$

$MN^2 = AM^2 + AN^2 - 2 \cdot AM \cdot AN \cdot \cos(\angle AMN)$
$MN^2 = 8^2 + 8^2 - 2 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 \cdot \cos(60^\circ)$
$MN^2 = 64 + 64 - 64 \cdot \frac{1}{2}$

$Area(\triangle BNM) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot BM \cdot MN = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 4\sqrt{6} = 8\sqrt{6}$

- $\triangle AMN$ is an isosceles triangle with base $MN = 4\sqrt{6}$ and height $AM = 8$. Therefore, its area is:

$Area(\triangle AMN) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot MN \cdot AM = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4\sqrt{6} \cdot 8 = 16\sqrt{6}$

$Area(\triangle BMN) = Area(\triangle BNM) + Area(\triangle AMN) = 8\sqrt{6} + 16\sqrt{6} = 24\sqrt{6}$
