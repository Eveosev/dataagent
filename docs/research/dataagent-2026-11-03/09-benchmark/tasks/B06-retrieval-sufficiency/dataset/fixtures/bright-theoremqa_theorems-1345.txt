## Essential Problem:

The problem is to calculate the total change in energy experienced when traversing a circular path of radius 2 units around the center of an energy field. We need to consider the strength of the energy field at different points along the path and integrate it over the entire circumference.

## Relevant Information:

* **Energy field strength formula:** This formula describes how the energy field strength varies with distance from the center. We need this formula to calculate the energy at each point on the circular path.
* **Circular path:** The path is a perfect circle with a radius of 2 units. Knowing the path allows us to determine the distance from the center at any point.
* **Calculus:**  We can use calculus, specifically integration, to sum the energy changes along the path.

## Step-by-Step Reasoning:

1. **Discretize the path:** We can imagine the circular path being divided into infinitesimally small segments. Each segment represents a tiny displacement along the path.
2. **Calculate energy change for each segment:** Using the energy field strength formula and the distance from the center for each segment, we can calculate the change in energy experienced while traversing that segment.
3. **Sum the energy changes:** We sum the energy changes for all the infinitesimal segments using integration. This provides the total change in energy for the entire circular path.
