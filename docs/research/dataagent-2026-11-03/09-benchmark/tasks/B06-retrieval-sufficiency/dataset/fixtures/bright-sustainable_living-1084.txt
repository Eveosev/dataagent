3. **Locally available plants:** Identifying plants commonly found in tropical regions that possess repellent qualities is important for accessibility and practicality.

## Exploring Alternatives:

Here are some potential avenues to explore:

**1. Other plants with repellent properties:**

* **Lemongrass:**  This popular culinary herb contains citronella, a natural mosquito repellent. 
* **Lavender:**  The scent of lavender is known to repel mosquitoes. 
* **Peppermint:**  The strong aroma of peppermint can deter mosquitoes.
* **Neem:**  Neem oil is a natural insecticide and repellent with proven efficacy against mosquitoes.
* **Citronella geranium:**  This geranium species is known for its strong citronella scent. 
* **Tulasi (Holy Basil):**  This sacred plant in India is believed to repel mosquitoes and has medicinal properties.
* **Catnip:**  While attractive to cats, catnip contains nepetalactone, which is ten times more effective than DEET in repelling mosquitoes.

**2. Combining plant-based repellents:**

Mixing different plant-based repellents may enhance their effectiveness and create a more pleasant scent.

**3. Utilizing essential oils:**

Extracting and diluting essential oils from mosquito-repelling plants can be applied to skin or clothing.

**4.  Consulting local experts:**

Engaging with local herbalists or botanists can provide valuable insights into native plants with mosquito-repelling properties.

**5. Researching new developments:**

Staying updated on the latest research in mosquito repellent technology and sustainable solutions is important.
