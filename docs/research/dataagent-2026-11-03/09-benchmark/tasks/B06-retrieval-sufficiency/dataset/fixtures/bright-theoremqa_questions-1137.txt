## Comparing the Brightness of Two Light Bulbs

### 1. Identifying the Essential Problem

The essential problem is to determine which light bulb appears brighter to the human eye based on their HSI color settings. 

### 2. Relevant Information and Reasoning

To address this problem, we need to consider the following:

* **HSI Color Model:** HSI stands for Hue, Saturation, and Intensity. 
    * Hue represents the color itself (e.g., red, green, blue).
    * Saturation represents the purity or "depth" of the color.
    * Intensity represents the brightness of the color.
* **Human Brightness Perception:** Human perception of brightness is not linear with respect to intensity. We are more sensitive to changes in brightness at lower intensities compared to higher intensities.
