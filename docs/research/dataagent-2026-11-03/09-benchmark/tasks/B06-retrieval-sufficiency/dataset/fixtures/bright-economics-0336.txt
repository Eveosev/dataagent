## Essential Problem:

The essential problem is to determine whether Gaza could be one of the wealthiest states on the Mediterranean if it received US$8,300 per capita in international aid, assuming no diversion of funds. 

## Relevant Information:

* **Total aid received:** We need to determine the total amount of aid Gaza has received over time. 
* **Population of Gaza:** We need to know the population of Gaza to calculate the per capita aid.
* **Economic potential of Gaza:** We need to analyze the economic potential of Gaza, including its resources, infrastructure, and human capital.
* **Comparison with other Mediterranean states:** We need to compare Gaza's economic potential with other Mediterranean states to assess its relative wealth.
* **Impact of political and security situation:** We need to consider the impact of the ongoing Israeli-Palestinian conflict and the internal political situation on Gaza's economy.

## Addressing the Question:

**Step 1: Calculating total aid received:** 

* We need to find reliable sources to determine the total amount of aid Gaza has received since its establishment. 
* This information can be found from reports by international organizations, such as the World Bank, the United Nations, and the Palestinian Authority.

**Step 2: Calculating per capita aid:**

* Once we have the total aid amount, we can divide it by the population of Gaza to calculate the per capita aid.
* The population figures can be obtained from the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics.

**Step 3: Assessing Gaza's economic potential:**

* We need to analyze Gaza's economic potential, considering its resources, infrastructure, and human capital.
* This involves examining factors such as its land area, natural resources, access to water, transportation infrastructure, educational attainment of its population, and skilled workforce availability.

**Step 4: Comparison with other Mediterranean states:**

* We need to compare Gaza's economic potential with other Mediterranean states to assess its relative wealth.
* This involves comparing factors such as GDP per capita, income inequality, and economic diversification.

**Step 5: Considering the political and security situation:**

* We need to consider the impact of the ongoing Israeli-Palestinian conflict and the internal political situation on Gaza's economy.
* This involves analyzing factors such as the impact of blockades, restrictions on movement, and internal political instability on economic activity and investment.

**Step 6: Conclusion:**

* Based on the analysis of all these factors, we can reach a conclusion on whether Gaza could be one of the wealthiest states on the Mediterranean if it received US$8,300 per capita in international aid, assuming no diversion of funds.

## Additional Considerations:

* The effectiveness of aid utilization:  It's important to consider how effectively the aid has been utilized in Gaza. 
* Corruption and mismanagement of funds can significantly impact the development outcomes.
* The long-term sustainability of economic growth: Even if Gaza receives substantial aid, it's crucial to ensure the created wealth is sustainable and not solely reliant on external assistance.

## Important Note:

It's important to acknowledge that the situation in Gaza is complex and multifaceted. Attributing its economic challenges solely to the amount of aid received or the actions of any single entity would be an oversimplification.
