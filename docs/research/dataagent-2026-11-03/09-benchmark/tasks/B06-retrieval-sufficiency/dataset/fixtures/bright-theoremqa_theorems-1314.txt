2. **Goal:** We need to find the y-position at x=-1.
3. **Plan:** We can use the information about the y-positions at x=1, x=2, and x=3 to solve for the coefficients a, b, and c in the equation f(x). Then, we can plug in x=-1 into the equation to find the corresponding y-position.
