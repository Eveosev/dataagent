## Essential Problem:

The essential problem is determining whether the infinite series representing the total rewards converges to a finite value. In other words, will the sum of the rewards from all the levels, calculated as described, approach a specific limit as the number of levels tends to infinity?

## Relevant Information:

To address this problem, we need to consider the following:

* **The formula for calculating the reward for each level:** reward = (level number)^2 * ln(level number) / (level number)!
* **The concept of infinite series:** An infinite series is the sum of infinitely many terms.
* **Convergence tests for infinite series:** Various tests exist to determine whether an infinite series converges to a finite value.

## Reasoning and Approach:

1. **Analyze the formula:** 
    * The term (level number)^2 grows rapidly as the level number increases.
    * The term ln(level number) grows more slowly than (level number)^2.
    * The term (level number)! grows much faster than both (level number)^2 and ln(level number).
2. **Apply the ratio test:** This test states that if the limit of the absolute value of the ratio of consecutive terms is less than 1, the series converges. Conversely, if the limit is greater than 1, the series diverges.
3. **Calculate the limit:**
    lim (n->∞) |(a(n+1))/(a(n))| = lim (n->∞) |((n+1)^2 * ln(n+1) / (n+1)!) / ((n)^2 * ln(n) / (n)!)|
    = lim (n->∞) |((n+1)/n)^2 * ln(n+1) / ln(n) * (n!)/(n+1)!)|
    = lim (n->∞) |(1 + 1/n)^2 * ln(1 + 1/n) / ln(n) * 1/n|
    = 1 * 1 * 0 = 0

4. **Conclusion:** Since the limit of the absolute value of the ratio of consecutive terms is 0, which is less than 1, the ratio test implies that the infinite series representing the total rewards converges. Therefore, the total sum of your rewards from all these levels will be a finite number.

## Additional Thoughts:

* Although the series converges, the actual value of the sum might be very large due to the rapid growth of the reward formula in the initial levels.
* Other convergence tests could be applied to confirm the result obtained with the ratio test.
* It's important to note that this analysis only considers the mathematical formula and doesn't take into account any potential limitations within the game itself (e.g., maximum level achievable).
