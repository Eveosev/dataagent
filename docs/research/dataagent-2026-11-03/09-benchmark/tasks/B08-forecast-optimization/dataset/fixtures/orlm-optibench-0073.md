A company produces four types of gadgets: G1, G2, G3, and G4, and a new type of gadget G5. They need to determine the quantities of each gadget to produce. The revenue per unit, production time per unit, and material cost per unit for each gadget are given in the following Table.

| Gadget | Revenue per Unit | Production Time per Unit | Material Cost per Unit |
|--------|------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| G1     | $50              | 2 hours                  | $20                    |
| G2     | $60              | 3 hours                  | $25                    |
| G3     | $70              | 4 hours                  | $30                    |
| G4     | $80              | 5 hours                  | $35                    |
| G5     | $90              | 6 hours                  | $40                    |

The company only has one production line and can only produce one gadget at a time. The company wants to maximize the profit efficiency (profit per hour of production time). The company has a limited production time of 100 hours. The company has a budget of $5000 for material costs. To meet market demand, the company needs to produce at least 40 gadgets in total.
