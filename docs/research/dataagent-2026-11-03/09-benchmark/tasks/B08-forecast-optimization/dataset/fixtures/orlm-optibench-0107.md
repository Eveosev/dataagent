A bakery produces four types of pastries: Croissant, Danish, Muffin, and Tart. The bakery needs to decide how many units of each pastry to produce for the upcoming holiday season. The selling price, cost of ingredients, and production time for each pastry are given in the following Table.

| Pastry  | Selling Price | Cost of Ingredients | Production Time |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Croissant | $3           | $1                  | 15 minutes      |
| Danish   | $4           | $2                  | 20 minutes      |
| Muffin   | $2.5         | $0.8                | 10 minutes      |
| Tart     | $5           | $3                  | 25 minutes      |

The bakery has a budget of $500 for ingredients. The bakery wants to produce at least 50 units of each pastry. The bakery has a total production time limit of 80 hours. The bakery has a limited production capacity and aims to maximize the total profit per hour of production.
